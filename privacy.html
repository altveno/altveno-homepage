Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

Altveno Ltd ("Altveno", "we", "us") operates the Altveno platform — a two-sided marketplace connecting institutional allocators with alternative investment fund managers. The platform is accessible via altveno.com, app.altveno.com, and the Altveno mobile app for iOS and Android.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, the legal basis for processing, and your rights. By using Altveno, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Altveno Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through the Altveno platform.

  • Company: Altveno Ltd
  • Registered office: United Kingdom
  • Contact: shiz.asif@altveno.com

2. What we collect

Account information

Full name, email address, role on the platform (allocator or fund manager), firm name, firm type, and headquarters location.

Firm and fund information

Firm description, website, inception date, team member details. Fund details: name, asset class, strategy, fee structure, terms, and performance history. Investment preferences (for allocators) including typical allocation sizes and asset class focus.

Communication data

Messages exchanged between connected users, meeting requests, responses and notes, and connection request status.

Document data

Due diligence questionnaires (DDQs) and supporting documents you upload, and access permissions you grant to other users.

Technical data

IP address, browser type, device information, log data (pages visited, features used, timestamps), and cookies and similar tracking technologies (see section 8).

Payment data (when billing is active)

Billing contact information, subscription plan and renewal status. Payment is processed by Stripe; we do not store full card details.

3. How we use your data

To provide the platform: account creation, authentication, displaying your firm and funds to other users, facilitating connections, enabling messaging and video meetings, processing DDQ workflows, storing performance data.

To improve the platform: analysing how features are used so we can fix bugs and prioritise improvements.

To communicate with you: sending transactional emails (connection requests, meeting reminders, password resets), responding to support enquiries, and (with separate consent) sending platform updates.

For legal and security purposes: detecting fraud and abuse, complying with regulatory obligations, defending legal claims.

For AI features: when you use Altveno AI features (peer analysis, DDQ pre-fill, allocator AI chat), we send relevant data to Anthropic Claude API for processing. We do not send raw personal data to AI providers beyond what is necessary to fulfil your request. AI providers do not retain data sent through our API beyond their processing window (currently zero retention with Anthropic).

4. Legal basis for processing (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

  • Contract performance: to deliver the platform services you have signed up for
  • Legitimate interest: to improve the platform, detect fraud, and communicate platform updates relevant to your account
  • Consent: for marketing communications and non-essential cookies (you can withdraw at any time)
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws (tax, anti-money-laundering, regulatory reporting)

5. Anonymity in peer benchmarking

The Altveno Peer Analysis and Altveno AI features display aggregated, anonymised performance data drawn from benchmark datasets. Individual managers are never identified in peer outputs. When data is aggregated across fewer than five managers in a strategy category, results are suppressed to prevent re-identification.

6. Who we share data with

Other platform users: information you choose to display on your firm profile (firm name, description, fund details, team) is visible to other users in accordance with the platform's visibility rules. Allocator AUM is shown as a range to unconnected managers and as an exact figure only after a connection is approved.

Service providers acting as data processors:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, real-time messaging) — hosted in EU
  • Vercel (web hosting)
  • Resend (transactional email)
  • Daily.co (video meeting rooms)
  • Anthropic (AI features — zero data retention)
  • Stripe (payment processing, when billing is active)
  • Expo / Apple / Google (mobile app distribution)

Legal and regulatory authorities where required by law.

We do not sell personal data to advertisers or any other third party.

7. International transfers

Data may be transferred outside the UK / European Economic Area when processed by our service providers (notably Anthropic, Stripe, Apple, Google). These transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or by equivalent safeguards.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Our web platform uses essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. The mobile app uses on-device storage for authentication tokens and preferences only.

9. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account:

  • Account and firm details are deleted within 30 days
  • Messages and meeting records are retained for 12 months in case of dispute, then deleted
  • Aggregated, anonymised performance data may be retained indefinitely for benchmarking purposes
  • Records required by law (e.g., tax records, subscription invoices) are kept for the period required by UK law (typically 6-7 years)

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention requirements
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances
  • Port your data to another service
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact shiz.asif@altveno.com. We will respond within one month.

11. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) for all data exchanged between your device and our servers
  • Encryption at rest for the database
  • Row-level security policies controlling who can read what data
  • Multi-factor authentication available for all accounts
  • Regular security reviews and dependency updates

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay.

12. Children

Altveno is a professional financial platform intended for use only by adults representing institutional firms. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be communicated by email or platform notification.
